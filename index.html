What can't be spokenmust be felt.

Across everything Ruben creates, one focus holds steady: drawing you in through sounds that tell you what words can’t.

Music only matters when it's felt, so it should carry emotion, pull you into the story being shown or heard, and make you feel like you're standing inside it.

Work & collaborations

Among his many credits, the most important are the Olympic documentary for two-time Olympian Howard Shu, the theatrical music for a Freek Vonk Studios show at AFAS Live in front of thousands of people, and a short teaser issued by the government of Rotterdam to draw attention to the city's skating scene.

Beyond these, his work spans hundreds of projects across film scores, trailers, advertisements and podcasts, and he has collaborated with award-winning composer and filmmaker John D. Boswell, known as Melodysheep, hosting a worldwide scoring competition for composers.
