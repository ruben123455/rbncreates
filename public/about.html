About

Costa Rican media composer, pianist and founder of The Composer Guide. He started making music at 11 on an iPad and began working professionally at 15.

Self-taught as a composer, with a degree in Electronic Music Production from SAE Institute and training under Mark Otten. He writes mainly in Logic Pro X with orchestral and cinematic libraries from Spitfire Audio, blending world music textures, orchestral writing and electronic production shaped by his Costa Rican upbringing. He divides his time between the Netherlands and Costa Rica.
