About

Ruben Camacho is a Costa Rican media composer for film, television, and media, and the founder of The Composer Guide, the largest dedicated composer community in the world. At 20, he is among the youngest composers to have built working relationships with the leading sample library and music technology brands in the industry.

His composing credits include an official Olympic campaign and documentary for two-time Olympic badminton player Howard Shu, a short film commissioned by the government of Rotterdam, and hundreds of musical projects spanning film scores, trailers, advertisements, and podcasts. He has collaborated with award-winning composer and filmmaker John D. Boswell, known as Melodysheep, hosting a worldwide scoring competition for composers.

Ruben holds ongoing partnerships with many of the most recognized names in music technology, including Spitfire Audio, EastWest Sounds, Vienna Symphonic Library, Orchestral Tools, ROLI, Expressive E, and SRM Sounds.

A pianist and self-taught composer, he began making music at the age of 11 on an iPad and started working professionally at 15. He later earned an Electronic Music Production degree from SAE Institute and trained under Mark Otten, the music coach behind Martin Garrix. He works primarily in Logic Pro X, drawing on an extensive collection of orchestral and cinematic tools, including Spitfire Audio’s Abbey Road, Air Studios, and Hans Zimmer collections, through his sponsorships with leading sample library developers.

His musical identity is shaped by his upbringing in Costa Rica, where early exposure to the country’s rich cultural and ethnic diversity gave him a distinctive sonic vocabulary. His scores weave world music textures with orchestral writing and electronic production, a blend that has become his signature.

Beyond composing, Ruben founded The Composer Guide, a platform and community that has grown to thousands of members worldwide, with hundreds of thousands of visitors. Through its content division, the platform has helped over 100 composers secure collaborations with brands including Spitfire Audio, Soundiron, Evolution Series, Eventide, and Sonarworks, all within its first six months of operation.

His work has been featured in Music Marcom, CanvasRebel, and BoldJourney.

Ruben divides his time between the Netherlands and Costa Rica.
